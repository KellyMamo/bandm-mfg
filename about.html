Our Story

Engineering Progress Across Africa

B&E began as a small engineering practice and grew into a full-scope EPC partner for energy, water, agriculture and infrastructure projects across the continent. Every project is approached with the same discipline: design it right, build it to last.

Today we deliver renewable energy, water and irrigation systems, and industrial automation for governments, NGOs, and private clients alike — backed by a delivery process that tracks every project from feasibility to commissioning. Our clients return to us because the work performs long after handover.
